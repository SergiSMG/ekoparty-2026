02 / The story in three decisions

Three things to take away

  1. 1

    Identity is a path,
    not a point

    Look beyond roles and direct permissions. Follow what the identity can actually reach.

    Reach
  2. 2

    Context turns signals
    into decisions

    Correlation, AI and analyst validation help determine what matters and when to act.

    Confidence
  3. 3

    Containment
    is not recovery

    Stopping the attacker is not enough. Trust must be restored and validated.

    Trust

Follow the path. Build the context. Restore the trust.
